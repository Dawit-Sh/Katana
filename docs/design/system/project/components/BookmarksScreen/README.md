# Bookmarks

Every book's marks in one place: its highlights (with their notes), its page bookmarks and its saved words. Katana's design for it is the Bookmarks canvas of 18 Nov 2026 (`Main`, `Bookmarks`, `BookPage`, `Reader`, `Idea-ShareQuote`, `Idea-Flashcards`); the code is `feature:annotations`.

- **In the menu** between Lists and Journal (`bookmark`), in the drawer and the rail. Picking it always lands on the books, like Settings on its hub.
- **No screen title.** The bar holds the menu button and, at its end, **Export all** (`AppButton`, `download`): every book's marks as one Markdown notebook, saved where the reader picks. Under the bar, a line counts it all: "6 books · 24 highlights · 5 bookmarks · 12 saved words", with **Review words** (`style`, text button) at its end while there are saved words.
- **Search** (`AppSearchField`, "Search bookmarks, highlights, notes and words") finds a book by its name, its author, or any of its marks' words: quotes, notes, saved words and their sentences, a bookmark's first words, its chapter and its place ("page 120", "120 of 300"), so a bookmark of a page with no words still turns up. Under a filter only that kind of mark is searched. Every search of marks (here, a book's page, the reader's marks panel) matches the same way (`MarkSearch`).
- **Filter chips** (`AppChip`): All, Highlights, Bookmarks, Words; then the order chip (`swap_vert`): Last marked or Title. The row scrolls sideways on a narrow window.
- **The books are covers** in the library's grid (its cover size and gap, `section-gap` 24 between rows). A book with bookmarks wears the amber ribbon at its cover's top end. Under the cover: the title (one line, SemiBold), pills with each count it has ("2 highlights" on the highlight yellow, "1 bookmark" on the ribbon amber, "1 word" on the word blue, at 28%), then "Last marked today, 18:38". A book the library no longer holds gets a typeset cover: its title and author in Literata on `surface-container-highest`.
- **A book's page** (tap a cover): the bar has Back and **Export** (`AppTonalButton`, that book). The cover (112 wide) beside the title in Literata Bold, the author, "2 highlights · 1 bookmark · 1 word" and **Continue · p. 8 of 78** (`AppButton`, opens the book where it was left). Then chips All and each kind with its count, an `AppSearchField` ("Search this book's marks", starting as the books' search when the book was found by one; "None of this book's marks match." when it finds nothing), then sections under `AppGroupLabel`s ("Bookmarks · 3" with its count), each mark an `AppPanel` row:
  - **Bookmarks**: the filled ribbon icon in amber, its name when it has one (then "Page 8 of 78" under it), else "Page 8 of 78 · 9%" (rounded as the reader rounds), the page's first words in Literata (two lines), its note in the note box, the chapter (left out when it only repeats the book's name) and when; **Name or note** (`edit`) and delete at the end. A tap opens the book on that page.
  - **Highlights**: a 4 wide bar in the highlight's colour, the quote in Literata, its note in a `surface-container-highest` box, then "Squiggle · Chapter 1 · today". A tap opens the book there.
  - **Saved words**: the word bold, its sentence in Literata, its note if any; Look up (`search`) and delete at the end.
- **Review words** (the flashcards): the bar has Back and "Review saved words"; under it "1 of 3 · from every book" and an `AppProgressBar`. The card (`AppPanel`, `dialog-inset` 24): "SAVED WORD", the word in Literata 52 Bold, its sentence in Literata italic with the word in `primary`, beside a 3 wide amber bar; the book, author and "saved 18 Nov"; then **Your note** ("Tap to add what it means, in your own words."; a tap opens an `AppDialog` with an `AppTextField`) and **Look it up** (`AppTonalButton`). Under the card **Again** (`AppTonalButton`) and **I know it** (`AppButton`), 56 tall, a half each, and the line "Words you know come back less often. Again brings it back later in this round."
  - A round takes the words that are due (never reviewed first, then the longest waiting). I know it rests a word for a day, then 3, 7, 14 and 30 days; Again puts it back at the start and to the back of the round. With nothing due, "Nothing to review today" offers **Review all words**; a finished round says how many it went through.
- **Empty**: "No bookmarks yet" with one line on how marks are made; a search or filter that finds nothing says "Nothing found".

## A bookmark's name and note

**Name or note** (`edit`) on a bookmark's row (the reader's marks panel, a book's page here) opens an `AppDialog`, "Name and note": a one-line `AppTextField` for the name ("Page 8 of 78" shows while it is empty) over a two-line one for the note ("A note, if you like"); Cancel and Save. Emptying a field removes it. Every list shows the name in the place of "Page 8 of 78" when there is one, and the note under the first words. Search matches both. On a Grimmory EPUB, both go to the server as the bookmark's title and notes; a bookmark not named here goes up named by its chapter, as Grimmory's web reader names its own, and that name coming back doesn't name it here.

## On the library's book page

The book page (the details sheet) has a **Bookmarks** section after its progress and Reading, before In your lists, for every book: an `AppSheetDivider`, the `AppSheetLabel` "Bookmarks · 3", then each bookmark in reading order as a 48-tall row inset 20: the amber filled ribbon icon (18), "Page 8 of 78 · 9%" (SemiBold), the page's first words (one line) and "Chapter 3 · today, 18:38". A tap closes the page and opens the book on that page. With none, one quiet line: "No bookmarks yet. While reading, tap the bookmark at the top to keep a page." The section appears once the book's bookmarks are read, so it never shows that line before the list.

## In the reader

- **The top bar** gains the bookmark button before the marks panel's: `bookmark`, filled (`bookmark` FILL 1) and in its selected container while the page on screen has a bookmark. A tap bookmarks the page (toast "Page bookmarked") or removes its bookmark ("Bookmark removed").
- **The ribbon**: while the page has a bookmark, an amber ribbon (26 × 46, a notch at its foot) hangs from the page's top edge, 72 in from the end. It stays when the bars hide. Scrolling (Pages: Scroll), the page is the screen: the ribbon hangs and the button is filled while a bookmark's place is between the screen's top and bottom, and a new bookmark keeps the place at the screen's top.
- **The marks panel** is named **Marks in this book** ("2 highlights · 1 bookmark · 1 word"; the button says "Highlights, bookmarks and words"). Every reader has it, the same panel (`BookMarksPanelBody`, `feature:annotations`), at the end edge, its button (`collections_bookmark`) after the bookmark button: EPUB, PDF, DjVu and comics (2026-11-20). A PDF or DjVu book counts its highlights and bookmarks, a comic its bookmarks. Highlights sit under their chapter (a PDF's under "Page 12"), and the list opens at the marks around the reader's place, the chapter's heading showing. Under the heading, once the book has a mark, an `AppSearchField` ("Search this book's marks"; "Nothing in this book's marks matches."). Its sections: Bookmarks ("Bookmarks · 3"; "Page 8 of 78", the first words), Highlights, Saved words. Its foot is one full-width `AppTonalButton`, **Export highlights** (this book). Every book's export is on the Bookmarks page.
- **The highlight card** gains **Share as an image** (`share`) between Copy and Delete. It opens the share card (`AppDialogCard`, "Share as an image"): the preview, then "Card" with four grounds (Paper, Dusk, Teal, and the book's cover colours) and Square or Tall (4:5); **Save image** (`AppTonalButton`, Pictures/Katana; Android 9 asks for storage once) and **Share** (`AppButton`, Android's share sheet). The image is 1080 wide: an accent quote mark, the quote in Literata italic as large as fits, the book and author bottom left, 刀 Katana bottom right in the accent.
- **Undo on a removal**: "Bookmark removed" and "Highlight removed" toasts carry **Undo** (the toast's accent pill, 6 s), in an EPUB, a PDF, DjVu or comic, and on the Bookmarks page; removed from an open marks panel, the toast shows over the panel (an open reader panel hosts the app's toasts). It brings the mark back as it was: the same mark (its id), place, words, name, note, style and colour. Back on a synced book it waits for the next sync like any change: if Grimmory heard of the removal meanwhile, the sync makes it there again.
- **Previous and next through marks**: opening a bookmark or highlight from a list (the Bookmarks page, the book page's Bookmarks, the marks panel) shows a strip over the dock's row, as a search's matches do: the mark's icon (`bookmark`, or `collections_bookmark` for a highlight), its name, chapter and page or the quote (one line), "3 of 12" under it, then Previous mark, Next mark (`navigate_before`, `navigate_next`) and Close (`close`). Previous and next go to the mark before or after it in the book, bookmarks and highlights together in reading order, and stop at either end; each jump offers "Back to page 8" like any jump. A saved word opened from the panel shows no strip. In a PDF, DjVu or comic the strip sits over the page strip and steps through the bookmarks (and a PDF's or DjVu book's highlights). The search strip wins while a search is on.

## The side menu (2026-11-21)

The drawer (360 wide with the reading card, 320 without; 360 on a phone held sideways, where Sources and Your reading sit side by side in slightly tighter rows, 12 at their sides) **never scrolls and nothing in it is cut off**. Top to bottom (Katana Desk's sidebar is the same):

- **Name**: Katana's launcher icon (26, `inner` radius), "Katana 刀" (16 SemiBold, 刀 Regular in `on-surface-variant`) and the **Settings gear** at the end (40), on the selected fill while Settings is open, with a dot while an update waits. Settings is no longer a row.
- **Reading card** (`JournalReadingCard`, `feature:journal`): an `AppPanel` on `surface-container`, `radius-container` 20, 12 inside and between its parts. "1 h 12 read today" ("22 min", "0 min"; the time 17 SemiBold, the rest 12.5 in `on-surface-variant`) with "Journal ›" at its end, the whole line opening the Journal. The book being read (read last and not finished, from any source): its cover 36 × 54, the title in the book face (15), "Stave Three · 34%" (or only "34%"), a 3 tall bar; a tap opens it. Then **the heat map** across the card's width: 16 weeks as columns, Monday on top, the months over the columns their 1st falls in, today ringed in `on-surface` (1.5, 1 clear of it), the days to come left out; squares up to 18 with 3 between, a tap opens the Journal. Nothing else: no streak, totals, key or last 7 days.
- **Sources**: a small label (12 SemiBold, `on-surface-variant`), then every source and "Add a server" as `AppDrawerRow`s (42 tall, growing with large text; pill shape; icon 22; 14.5 Medium), each source with its book count at the end as its library counts them (Kavita and Stump: series and loose issues), once that source has loaded.
- **Your reading**: Lists, Bookmarks, My words (with its count), Journal.
- The free room, then **Word of the day** (`WordOfTheDayCard`, `feature:annotations`), on the same panel (12 inside, 14 at the start): "Word of the day" and, at the end, the word's progress dot (a ring for new, half for learning, full in the reading-day green once learned) and its book; the word in the book face (20); its meaning on one line (its own note, else the built-in dictionary's); then **Review** (filled pill 32: the review on this word alone), **I know it** (tonal pill: a good review in My words' own schedule; "✓ Known" once answered today) and **→** (32 circle, another word; only with more than one). A word from My words picked by the date, the same all day; → steps to the next in the day's order and that stays for the day. Hidden while My words is empty.

**When the height is short** (`fitDrawer`, `DrawerFitLayout`): first the heat map's squares get smaller (down to 8; all 16 weeks stay, since a week is a column and fewer weeks would not make it shorter; weeks drop only where even 8 squares can't fit the width), then the word card leaves out its meaning, then the word card goes (the heat map grows back into its room), then the reading card. From 150% text the reading card is left out, as before.

**Reading days by a daily goal** (`ReadingGoal`, `core:model`): 30 min a day unless set in Settings › Reading › Daily reading goal or under the Journal's calendars ("Goal 30 min a day ▾"; 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90 or 120). Five steps: no reading, under half the goal, under the goal, the goal met, twice the goal or more. The shades (`readingDayShades`) are the theme's `readingDay` colour (Katana's green: Paper's read green, a lighter one in Graphite; a character theme's own accent) at 28, 52 and 78% over the panel and at full strength, with a faint `outline-variant` square for no reading. The Journal's month and year calendars use the same steps and shades, with "Less … More" and the goal under them.
