# Bookmarks

Every book's marks in one place: its highlights (with their notes), its page bookmarks and its saved words. Katana's design for it is the Bookmarks canvas of 18 Nov 2026 (`Main`, `Bookmarks`, `BookPage`, `Reader`, `Idea-ShareQuote`, `Idea-Flashcards`); the code is `feature:annotations`.

- **In the menu** between Lists and Journal (`bookmark`), in the drawer and the rail. Picking it always lands on the books, like Settings on its hub.
- **No screen title.** The bar holds the menu button and, at its end, **Export all** (`AppButton`, `download`): every book's marks as one Markdown notebook, saved where the reader picks. Under the bar, a line counts it all: "6 books · 24 highlights · 5 bookmarks · 12 saved words", with **Review words** (`style`, text button) at its end while there are saved words.
- **Search** (`AppSearchField`, "Search bookmarks, highlights, notes and words") finds a book by its name, its author, or any of its marks' words: quotes, notes, saved words and their sentences, a bookmark's first words, its chapter and its place ("page 120", "120 of 300"), so a bookmark of a page with no words still turns up. Under a filter only that kind of mark is searched. Every search of marks (here, a book's page, the reader's marks panel) matches the same way (`MarkSearch`).
- **Filter chips** (`AppChip`): All, Highlights, Bookmarks, Words; then the order chip (`swap_vert`): Last marked or Title. The row scrolls sideways on a narrow window.
- **The books are covers** in the library's grid (its cover size and gap, `section-gap` 24 between rows). A book with bookmarks wears the amber ribbon at its cover's top end. Under the cover: the title (one line, SemiBold), pills with each count it has ("2 highlights" on the highlight yellow, "1 bookmark" on the ribbon amber, "1 word" on the word blue, at 28%), then "Last marked today, 18:38". A book the library no longer holds gets a typeset cover: its title and author in Literata on `surface-container-highest`.
- **A book's page** (tap a cover): the bar has Back and **Export** (`AppTonalButton`, that book). The cover (112 wide) beside the title in Literata Bold, the author, "2 highlights · 1 bookmark · 1 word" and **Continue · p. 8 of 78** (`AppButton`, opens the book where it was left). Then chips All and each kind with its count, an `AppSearchField` ("Search this book's marks", starting as the books' search when the book was found by one; "None of this book's marks match." when it finds nothing), then sections under `AppGroupLabel`s ("Bookmarks · 3" with its count), each mark an `AppPanel` row:
  - **Bookmarks**: the filled ribbon icon in amber, "Page 8 of 78 · 9%" (rounded as the reader rounds), the page's first words in Literata (two lines), the chapter (left out when it only repeats the book's name) and when; delete at the end. A tap opens the book on that page.
  - **Highlights**: a 4 wide bar in the highlight's colour, the quote in Literata, its note in a `surface-container-highest` box, then "Squiggle · Chapter 1 · today". A tap opens the book there.
  - **Saved words**: the word bold, its sentence in Literata, its note if any; Look up (`search`) and delete at the end.
- **Review words** (the flashcards): the bar has Back and "Review saved words"; under it "1 of 3 · from every book" and an `AppProgressBar`. The card (`AppPanel`, `dialog-inset` 24): "SAVED WORD", the word in Literata 52 Bold, its sentence in Literata italic with the word in `primary`, beside a 3 wide amber bar; the book, author and "saved 18 Nov"; then **Your note** ("Tap to add what it means, in your own words."; a tap opens an `AppDialog` with an `AppTextField`) and **Look it up** (`AppTonalButton`). Under the card **Again** (`AppTonalButton`) and **I know it** (`AppButton`), 56 tall, a half each, and the line "Words you know come back less often. Again brings it back later in this round."
  - A round takes the words that are due (never reviewed first, then the longest waiting). I know it rests a word for a day, then 3, 7, 14 and 30 days; Again puts it back at the start and to the back of the round. With nothing due, "Nothing to review today" offers **Review all words**; a finished round says how many it went through.
- **Empty**: "No bookmarks yet" with one line on how marks are made; a search or filter that finds nothing says "Nothing found".

## On the library's book page

The book page (the details sheet) has a **Bookmarks** section after its progress and Reading, before In your lists, for every book: an `AppSheetDivider`, the `AppSheetLabel` "Bookmarks · 3", then each bookmark in reading order as a 48-tall row inset 20: the amber filled ribbon icon (18), "Page 8 of 78 · 9%" (SemiBold), the page's first words (one line) and "Chapter 3 · today, 18:38". A tap closes the page and opens the book on that page. With none, one quiet line: "No bookmarks yet. While reading, tap the bookmark at the top to keep a page." The section appears once the book's bookmarks are read, so it never shows that line before the list.

## In the reader

- **The top bar** gains the bookmark button before the marks panel's: `bookmark`, filled (`bookmark` FILL 1) and in its selected container while the page on screen has a bookmark. A tap bookmarks the page (toast "Page bookmarked") or removes its bookmark ("Bookmark removed").
- **The ribbon**: while the page has a bookmark, an amber ribbon (26 × 46, a notch at its foot) hangs from the page's top edge, 72 in from the end. It stays when the bars hide. Scrolling (Pages: Scroll), the page is the screen: the ribbon hangs and the button is filled while a bookmark's place is between the screen's top and bottom, and a new bookmark keeps the place at the screen's top.
- **The marks panel** is named **Marks in this book** ("2 highlights · 1 bookmark · 1 word"; the button says "Highlights, bookmarks and words"). Under the heading, once the book has a mark, an `AppSearchField` ("Search this book's marks"; "Nothing in this book's marks matches."). Its sections: Bookmarks ("Bookmarks · 3"; "Page 8 of 78", the first words), Highlights, Saved words. Its foot is one full-width `AppTonalButton`, **Export highlights** (this book). Every book's export is on the Bookmarks page.
- **The highlight card** gains **Share as an image** (`share`) between Copy and Delete. It opens the share card (`AppDialogCard`, "Share as an image"): the preview, then "Card" with four grounds (Paper, Dusk, Teal, and the book's cover colours) and Square or Tall (4:5); **Save image** (`AppTonalButton`, Pictures/Katana; Android 9 asks for storage once) and **Share** (`AppButton`, Android's share sheet). The image is 1080 wide: an accent quote mark, the quote in Literata italic as large as fits, the book and author bottom left, 刀 Katana bottom right in the accent.

## The menu's reading card

The drawer (360 wide with the card, 320 without) holds a reading card in the room between the sources and Lists, centred there; it scrolls with the sources on a short window. It is an `AppPanel` (16 at the sides, 8 top and bottom and between its parts, tight enough for the Fire's 1024-tall menu under its name and sources without scrolling), from the journal's own reading sessions, and a tap opens the Journal.

- "Reading" (Bold) over "Last 15 weeks, 10 Aug to today", and "Journal ›" in `primary` at its end.
- Three tiles on `surface-container-highest`: the **streak** in days (`local_fire_department`, amber; it still counts while today has no reading yet), **days read** (`event_available`) and books **finished** (`task_alt`) in the 15 weeks.
- **The heat map**: 15 columns of Monday to Sunday, the last one this week, tiles up to 13 square with 3 between, their size shrinking to fit; Mon, Wed, Fri and Sun beside them; the months over the columns that hold their 1st. A tile's shade is the minutes read that day (none; up to 20, 40, 70; more) as `primary` at 30, 50, 75 and 100% over the empty tile; today has an `on-surface` ring; the days after today are outlined only.
- "Best day: Sun 15 Nov, 1h 42m" with the Less … More key, then an `AppDivider`.
- **Last 7 days** with their total ("· 4h 29m") and "38 min a day"; seven bars up to 20 tall, each with its time over it and its day under it, today's in `primary` and named Today.
