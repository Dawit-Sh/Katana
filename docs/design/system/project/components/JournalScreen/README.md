# Journal

The journal's month or year: the period with its arrows and Month / Year, then its panels in three groups, each under an `AppGroupLabel`: **Time**, **Books** and **Taste**.

- **No "Journal" eyebrow.** The period, "October 2026", is the first content and stays as the title: it is the content, and its arrows move it.
- **Gutter 24** from Medium, 16 on phones; content top 84; bottom 24. Panels use `inset` 20 at every width, `grid-gap` 16 between panels and rows, `section-gap` 24 between groups.
- **Rows, never columns.** Where the journal is 880 wide or more, a group is rows on a 12-column grid and every row ends flush: one big panel with up to two small ones stacked beside it, the stack as tall as the big panel (or the big panel as tall as the stack). Below 880 every panel takes the full width, in the same order.
  - **Time:** the summary; then Reading days (7) beside When you read over Pages per day (5). The year: the year's Reading days (8) beside Reading time by month over Pages per month (4).
  - **Books:** Highlights this month (12, the month only); then What you read (7) beside Finished over Needs rating (5); then Lists you finished. The year: Finished (8) beside Needs rating (4); then Lists you finished.
  - **Taste:** Ratings (4) beside Most read (8).
- **A panel with nothing in it steps aside**, and what is left in its row widens to fill it; a row with nothing goes, and so does a group. A period with nothing at all shows one empty state.
- **The summary's rows end flush:** every figure in one row where they fit (all five on Expanded), else rows of three, two on phones, the cells of a shorter last row widening to fill it.
- **Charts** are 72 tall at least and grow with their panel; **the month's calendar is small squares** (34, 6 apart, `radius` 8, the day's number in its top corner at 11): from Medium beside "Reading days", "Best day: 18 Nov, 8 min" and the key (Less, the five shades, More; "Finished something" with its dot under it when the month has one; then "Goal 30 min a day ▾", which picks the daily goal), so the month is the height of its squares; on phones under the heading, a seventh of the width, no bigger than 44. A day is shaded by its minutes against the daily goal, the side menu's scale (see BookmarksScreen, "The side menu"): no reading a faint `outline-variant` square, then under half the goal, under the goal, the goal met, twice the goal or more in the theme's reading-day green; a day to come only an outline, today ringed; the year's months go four to a row, three on phones, with the same key and goal.
- **When you read** draws your usual hours in `primary`, the reading days' colour, and the rest in `on-surface-variant` at 45%, with a key under the bars (a 10 square, `radius` 3, per colour: "Your usual hours", "Other hours").
- **Highlights this month** ("Highlights in October" for a month gone by): "2 highlights · 1 saved word, all in The Hole" ("in 3 books" across several), then the latest two of either kind, each behind a 3 wide bar: a highlight's words in the book face between quotes, in its own colour, over "The Hole · 18 Nov"; a saved word in 15 SemiBold, the bar in `tertiary`, over "Saved word · The Hole". "See all in Bookmarks" (`primary` text button with `navigate_next`) closes it once the Bookmarks page exists. A month without either has no panel.
- What you read shows the longest six, then Show all; "Opened for a minute or two" closes the panel. Book rows are `AppBookRow`; Finished and Needs rating are rows of covers that scroll sideways.
