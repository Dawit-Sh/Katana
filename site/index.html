Katana刀

A simple Android reader for your own book server.

I made Katana because I wanted a nice way to read the books on my Grimmory server, on my old Fire tablet that I use for reading only, and on my phone. It's a fork of yabai with some things that I added because I was missing them. This is basically my reading app that just suits my needs and has the tools I want to have whenever I'm reading digitally.

Download the latest version

For Android 9 or newer

Other versions of the same app:
  • 64-bit: almost any phone or tablet from the last several years (Pixel, Samsung and so on)
  • Fire tablets and older 32-bit devices
  • Universal: works everywhere, just bigger. Pick this one if you're not sure

The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser. Katana isn't on the Play Store, so Play Protect may warn you too. Once it's installed, Katana updates itself from Settings › About, and an update keeps your books and highlights.

What it does

  • Reads your books from Grimmory, and also Kavita and Stump, or from files on your device.
  • EPUB, PDF, comics (CBZ and CBR), MOBI, AZW3, FB2 and DjVu.
  • Highlights and notes in four styles, synced both ways with Grimmory, so they show up in its web reader and in KOReader too.
  • Your reading place syncs with the server, and books download for offline reading, picking up where they stopped if the Wi-Fi drops.
  • Save words and go over them as flashcards, bookmark pages, search inside a book, and share a highlight as a quote card.
  • Export your highlights and notes as Markdown, and back everything up to a file.
  • Works away from home too, over something like Tailscale.
  • Runs on old Amazon Fire tablets and on new phones alike.

What it isn't

Katana isn't trying to be a reader with every feature piled in. It does what I need, and I'll keep fixing what breaks. If you like it but want something it doesn't do, fork it and make it yours, with whatever AI agent you like to work with. That's pretty much how Katana started.
